Voice Commands

  • "Locate current address" Finds the current location and reads the detected address aloud.
  • "Locate current address to home" Starts spoken guidance to the saved home destination.
  • "Set location" Uses microphone access to update the current address in Location Details.
  • "Navigate home" Begins navigation toward home.
  • "Repeat instruction" Replays the current spoken instruction.
  • "Stop navigation" Ends the route guidance.

How Echopath Guides

  • 1. Listen Tap the microphone and speak naturally.
  • 2. Locate Echopath finds your current position and announces it.
  • 3. Navigate The assistant provides spoken turn-by-turn directions.
  • 4. Confirm Say repeat, next, left, or right to continue.
Echopath is an accessible voice navigation prototype for blind and low-vision users.
